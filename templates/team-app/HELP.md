## Getting started

The app is new: the team builds it from what you ask for. Press the round
chat button in the corner of the page and say what you need in your own
words, such as a page to keep a list, a form, or a report. The team's lead
asks what it needs to know, then shows you a version to try.

If the button says Help rather than chat, ask the team in its Slack channel
for your own link: type the team's /hi command there.
