      People
      
                  
      Who may do what. Nobody has a level for being in the app's channel: an owner gives it. Pro Devs build and release as owners do, with Chat, terminal, VS Code, and SSH, but don't roll back, and don't change production's data or settings, the app's keys, the sites the dev box reaches, the addresses, or who is who. Vibe Devs build with the agents in Chat only, and release. Contributors report bugs as tasks, to the lead in Slack or from the app's helper with their own link. Anyone else can use the app and ask the lead questions.
