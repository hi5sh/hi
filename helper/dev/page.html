            People
            
          
            Who may do what. Nobody has a level for being in the team's channel: an owner gives it. Developers build as owners do, but don't release or roll back, and don't change production's data or settings, the team's keys, the sites the dev box reaches, the addresses, or who is who. Contributors report bugs as tasks, to the lead in Slack or from the app's helper with their own link. Anyone else can use the app and ask the lead questions.
